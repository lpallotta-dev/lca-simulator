System boundary
What does cradle-to-gate mean? The assessment covers the production chain from raw material extraction to the finished component leaving the manufacturing facility. Raw materials → Feedstock production → Manufacturing → Finishing Product use and end-of-life are outside this boundary. This study also excludes intermediate transportation.

Study framework

ISO 14040/14044 framework · SimaPro 10.5 · Ecoinvent 3.12 · ReCiPe 2016, Hierarchist perspective.

System boundary

Cradle-to-gate manufacturing assessment. Intermediate transportation, use and end-of-life stages are excluded.

Comparison basis

One finished punch with the specified geometry. Batch impacts are divided by the number of parts to obtain per-component results.

Data sources

SolidWorks CAM simulations, WAAM measurements, CENTIMFE SLM data and simulations, literature inputs and background datasets.

CNC route

ER70S6 steel stock.

Steel production → hot rolling → CNC milling

WAAM route

ER70S6 wire with subtractive finishing.

Steel production → hot rolling → wire drawing → WAAM → CNC finishing

SLM route

1.2709 steel powder with subtractive finishing.

Steel production → gas atomization → SLM → CNC finishing

Source: thesis Chapters 3–4. The dashboard is a presentation of reported results; it does not rerun SimaPro or calculate a new LCA.
